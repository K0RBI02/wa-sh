In apartment buildings the washer and dryer usually live in the basement – and then you forget them. wa:sh sets the timer, reminds you in time and tells you when to head down.

<b>As simple as this</b>
1. Start – pick the machine, a favourite program or a custom time
2. Relax – time left and progress always in view, a reminder 10 minutes before
3. Down to the basement – a notification as soon as it's done, and a nudge in case you doze off

<b>Also</b>
• Washer and dryer run independently – even at the same time
• Persistent “running” display with countdown and progress bar, a Live Update on Android 16
• Notifications per machine, every kind adjustable on its own
• Quick start right from the app icon
• Optional: Home Assistant integration
• No ads, no account, no trackers

The app is currently available in German.
